Help

What do I get for free?

Stages 1 and 2 (10 units, their projects, the first solo project, the debugger, Pong, Snake, Minesweeper, the sorting visualizer and the first Block World builds), the Lab, and the free Dojo puzzles. The free version has ads.

What's in PyForge Pro?

Stages 3 to 5, every game and Block World build, every Dojo puzzle, unlimited practice, certificates for every stage, and no ads. Monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PyForge. Or in the app: Me → PyForge Pro → Manage.

I bought Pro on another device.

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does my code really run Python?

Yes, real Python 3.13 (CPython compiled to WebAssembly), with pandas, numpy, requests, BeautifulSoup, openpyxl and pypdf. It runs on your device, even offline.

Is it affiliated with any game company?

No. PyForge teaches Python and the scripting skills used by many game engines. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any game company.
